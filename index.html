Privacy Policy (WhatsApp Enquiry Assistant) Rimbun Capital Sdn. Bhd. (506942-D) This policy explains how Rimbun Capital Sdn. Bhd. ("we", "us") handles personal data when you contact our conveyancing department through our WhatsApp enquiry assistant. It is issued in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia ("PDPA"). 1. What this service is Our WhatsApp number is answered first by an automated assistant. It asks a few short questions to understand who you are, which property your enquiry concerns and what you need, and then passes your enquiry to the staff member in charge of that property. The assistant does not give legal advice and does not make decisions about your file. 2. Personal data we collect When you message us on WhatsApp, we collect: your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name; the messages you send us, and our replies; details you provide in the conversation: your name, whether you are a purchaser, lawyer, bank officer or other party, your firm or bank, the project name, unit number and location of the property, and a description of your enquiry; the date and time of each message. Voice notes, images and documents sent to the assistant are not opened or analysed by it. We record only that one was sent, so that our staff can follow up with you. Please do not send identity card numbers, bank account details, passwords or other sensitive information to the assistant. Our staff will tell you how to provide such information if it is needed. 3. Why we use your personal data We use your personal data to: respond to your enquiry and direct it to the right member of staff; contact you about your enquiry, including by WhatsApp; keep a record of enquiries received about each property; handle complaints and improve our service; comply with our legal and regulatory obligations. Providing this information is voluntary, but without it we may be unable to identify your file or respond to your enquiry. 4. Automated processing Your messages are processed by an artificial intelligence service so that the assistant can understand your enquiry and reply in your language. It is used only to collect the information described above and to pass your enquiry to our staff. Our staff, not the assistant, deal with your enquiry itself. 5. Who we share your personal data with We disclose your personal data only as needed for the purposes above, to: our staff who handle the property concerned, and their head of department; Meta Platforms, Inc., which operates WhatsApp and delivers messages between you and us; Anthropic, PBC, which provides the artificial intelligence service that processes your messages on our behalf; our hosting and IT service providers, who store the data on our behalf; regulators, courts or authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for marketing by third parties. 6. Transfer outside Malaysia Meta, Anthropic and our hosting providers may process personal data on servers outside Malaysia. By using this service you consent to that transfer. We use providers that commit to protecting the data they process for us. 7. How long we keep it We keep enquiry records for seven (7) years after the enquiry is closed, or longer where the law requires, and then delete or anonymise them. 8. Security We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including restricting access to authorised staff and protecting data in transit. WhatsApp messages are also subject to WhatsApp's own security and privacy terms. 9. Your rights Under the PDPA you may: request access to the personal data we hold about you; request correction of personal data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date; withdraw your consent or limit how we process your personal data; make an enquiry or complaint about how your personal data is handled. To do so, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity, and a fee may be charged for access requests as permitted by law. If you withdraw consent we may be unable to continue handling your enquiry through WhatsApp. 10. Contact us Rimbun Capital Sdn. Bhd.
